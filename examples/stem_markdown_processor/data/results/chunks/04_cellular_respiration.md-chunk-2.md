## Electron Transport Chain and Oxidative Phosphorylation

The electron transport chain consists of a series of protein complexes embedded in the inner mitochondrial membrane. Electrons transferred from NADH and FADH2 drive proton pumps, generating an electrochemical gradient across the membrane that powers ATP synthase.
