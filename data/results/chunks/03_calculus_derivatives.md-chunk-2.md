## Fundamental Theorem of Calculus

The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus establishes the connection between differentiation and integration. It asserts that integration and differentiation are inverse operations:

$$\int_{a}^{b} f(x) \, dx = F(b) - F(a)$$

where $F'(x) = f(x)$.
